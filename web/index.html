    How it works

    One model asked to grade a resume will happily grade its own work. HireLens splits the job between six agents so that each claim gets checked by someone else.

    Orchestrator
    Plans each run, reads every result, and changes the plan when something looks wrong: a resume that didn’t parse well, a job with no clear requirements, an agent that failed.
    Resume Parser
    Turns your PDF into skills, experience, projects and education, and says how confident it is.
    Job Analyst
    Separates what the role requires from what’s nice to have. If the post is vague, it searches the company’s careers page on its own.
    Matcher
    Scores skills, experience, projects, education and keywords, quoting your resume for every claim.
    Critic
    Checks every quote against your actual resume and sends inflated scores back to the matcher to revise.
    Career Coach
    Rewrites your bullets around the role, predicts interview questions, and suggests how to close each gap.

    An honest first check, not a guarantee of selection. Your resume is used for this analysis only and isn’t stored.
